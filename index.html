        How to move

        Click a cell to move your pawn, or a groove between cells to place a wall; on a touch screen tap a groove once to preview a wall and again to place it. With the keyboard, focus the board, move the cursor with the arrow keys, press H or V to choose a horizontal or vertical wall, P to go back to pawn moves, and Enter to play. Page Up and Page Down step back and forward through the game, and Home and End jump to its start and its latest position.
