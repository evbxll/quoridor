    Step through the game, or choose a move in the list, to see the network's reading of that position.

      The bar beside the board is the network's reading at 32 searches. The numbered markers are each bot's own estimate from its latest move.

      How to move

      Click a cell to move your pawn, or a groove between cells to place a wall; on a touch screen tap a groove once to preview a wall and again to place it. With the keyboard, focus the board, move the cursor with the arrow keys, press H or V to choose a horizontal or vertical wall, P to go back to pawn moves, and Enter to play.
